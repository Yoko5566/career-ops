# Application Notes

## Why AWS / Why TAM

The strongest application story is not "I am already an AWS engineer." It is:

I already operate in an enterprise-support model where I coordinate customers, account stakeholders, engineers, technical advisors, specialists, and management through complex incidents. The AWS TAM role extends that same customer-advocacy and technical-coordination responsibility into deeper cloud technical ownership.

## Qualification Risk Disclosure

Do not claim:
- 3+ years of hands-on AWS engineering
- Production AWS architecture ownership
- Three AWS technical domains unless evidence can be demonstrated
- AWS certifications not actually held
- Production software-engineering experience

Be prepared to explain relevant troubleshooting exposure honestly across:
- applications / endpoint and client issues
- identity and access
- networking/proxy/connectivity
- security-tool interactions
- cloud/service supportability

## Suggested Recruiter Answer — Why are you interested in this role?

I currently work in enterprise support operations for Microsoft services, where I coordinate complex customer incidents across CSAMs, support engineers, Technical Advisors, specialists, management stakeholders, and customer teams. The part of my role I am most interested in is understanding business impact, aligning the right technical resources, driving escalations through resolution, and maintaining trusted customer communication. AWS TAM is a natural next step because it combines those responsibilities with deeper cloud technical ownership and customer advocacy.

My direct professional experience is primarily in Microsoft enterprise support rather than AWS. I would not represent myself as having production AWS engineering ownership. My transferable experience is in enterprise incident management, cloud-service support coordination, identity and access scenarios, application/service troubleshooting, escalation management, and working closely with technical engineers and account stakeholders. I am actively building deeper cloud technical knowledge and would approach AWS-specific depth as the main development area for this transition.
